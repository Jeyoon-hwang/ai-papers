# FFAL v3.2: Scaling Real-Physics Affordance Learning to 10K Samples

**One-line:** at 10× data with a shared 6-layer residual head, FFAL does **not** beat DINOv2 on AUROC (0.8509 vs 0.8849), but it does win on output-FIS stability (0.9630 vs 0.9562). The v2 "+10 pp over DINOv2" claim, retracted in v3, stays retracted.

## Abstract

We scale the FFAL real-data pipeline from 1,020 to **10,000** MuJoCo affordance samples (100 procedurally generated objects × 100 episodes), parallelized to 10 workers and completing in 2.6 min wall-clock. All five comparison models (ResNet50, ViT-B/16, CLIP-B/32, DINOv2, FFAL) share a new 6-layer residual head (in→512→256→128→64→32→K, LayerNorm + GELU + Dropout 0.3, residual within each width plateau) and identical optimizer settings, so any gap between FFAL and DINOv2 cannot be attributed to architecture or capacity. On a 15-object held-out test split (1,500 unseen samples), **FFAL reaches AUROC 0.8509 vs DINOv2's 0.8849**, with output FIS 0.9630 vs 0.9562. Every number in this paper is reproducible from the four scripts in `scripts/` with seed `20260516`.

## 1. Method recap

- **Backbone**: frozen ImageNet/CLIP/DINOv2 pre-trained encoder. No fine-tuning.
- **Head**: `DeepResidualHead`, 6 layers, ~280K params. Identical for all 5 models.
- **FFAL extra**: cosine-similarity loss in *output* space across V augmented views, weighted by λ_FI = 0.5. The FI loss pulls the head's K-dim logit vector toward the same direction across image augmentations, encouraging functional invariance without changing the backbone or its features.

## 2. Data

- 100 procedurally-generated objects (5 shapes × 4 surface modifiers × 5 continuous variants).
- 100 episodes / object, each with its own camera & lighting jitter.
- 5 affordance probes (sittable, stackable, graspable, pushable, pourable) executed as actual MuJoCo rigid-body rollouts; labels are thresholded *after* the rollout.
- 2.59 min wall-clock for 10,000 samples on 10 CPU workers (10000 new PNGs).
- Class balance:
  - sittable: pos=3622 (36.2%), neg=6378
  - stackable: pos=4328 (43.3%), neg=5672
  - graspable: pos=2200 (22.0%), neg=7800
  - pushable: pos=4500 (45.0%), neg=5500
  - pourable: pos=905 (9.0%), neg=9095

## 5. Honest take-aways

1. **FFAL still does not beat DINOv2 even at 10K**: 0.8509 vs 0.8849. At this scale, the gap from v2's marketing claim is the data, not the FI loss.
2. **The leader is `dinov2` at AUROC 0.8849**.
3. **Output FIS is a different axis from AUROC.** Reporting both is the minimum a real-world deployment review should require, since augmentation stability and label accuracy can diverge.
4. **Data scaling is the biggest single lever** in this whole pipeline. Going from 1,020 → 10,000 samples moves the average AUROC by more than any head change we tried. If a reviewer wants further gains, the way forward is *more objects*, not *more clever losses*.
5. **Held-out objects, not held-out views.** v3 split 30 objects → 5 test; v3.2 splits 100 → 15 test. v3.2's numbers are a stricter generalization test, which is why DINOv2's raw AUROC drops slightly in absolute terms but the **relative** ranking and the FFAL-vs-DINOv2 comparison both become more trustworthy.
